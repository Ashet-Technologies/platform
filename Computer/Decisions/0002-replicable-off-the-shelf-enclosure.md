# 0002 — Replicable Off-the-Shelf Enclosure

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

The Ashet Home Computer enclosure is based primarily on an off-the-shelf case.

The stock front and rear faces are replaced with custom panels.

These replacement panels are designed to be laser-cut parts so they can be reproduced with commonly available fabrication tools and services.

Suitable fabrication options include:

- a local hackerspace or makerspace with a laser cutter
- an online laser-cutting service
- potentially FR-4 panel fabrication where appropriate

## Rationale

Using a stock enclosure avoids the cost and complexity of manufacturing a complete custom case.

Limiting the custom mechanical parts to flat, laser-cut front and rear panels keeps the computer mechanically reproducible without requiring injection molding, CNC machining, or other specialized manufacturing processes.

## Consequences

- The enclosure body can be sourced as a standard commercial part.
- Only the front and rear panels require project-specific fabrication.
- Replacement panels can be reproduced locally or ordered from common fabrication services.
- The mechanical design remains accessible to hobbyist and hackerspace-level manufacturing.
